# Privacy Policy

**TTS2Web**

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Welcome to TTS2Web! We understand the importance of your personal information and are fully committed to protecting its security and reliability. We strive to maintain your trust and adhere to the following principles to protect your personal information: consistency of powers and responsibilities, clarity of purpose, choice and consent, minimum necessity, security assurance, subject participation, and openness and transparency.

Please read and fully understand this Privacy Policy before using our products (or services).

### I. How We Collect and Use Your Information

This application (TTS2Web) itself **does not** actively collect, store, or share any of your personally identifiable information with third parties. All your note data is stored locally on your device, and we do not have access to it.

### II. Third-Party Services

To maintain the operation of the application, provide free services, and analyze app performance and user interactions to improve product experience, we have integrated the Google AdMob advertising service and Google Firebase Analytics service.

1.  **About Advertising and Analytics Services**
    We use the mobile advertising service (AdMob) and app analytics service (Firebase Analytics) provided by Google. These third-party service providers may collect and use some of your device and interaction data.

2.  **Data Collected by SDKs**
    The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Firebase Analytics SDK automatically collect certain data for purposes such as advertising, performance analytics, and product improvement. This data may include:
    *   **IP address**: May be used to estimate the general location of the device.
    *   **User product interactions**: Such as app launches, page views, feature usage, taps, and video views.
    *   **Diagnostic and performance information**: Information related to app and SDK performance, such as app launch time, crash logs, performance diagnostics, or energy usage.
    *   **Device and account identifiers**: Such as the Android advertising ID (ad ID), app instance ID, and device-related identifiers.

    All user data collected by Google SDKs is encrypted in transit using the Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocol.

3.  **Third-Party Privacy Policies**
    We recommend that you review Google's privacy policy and Firebase's privacy guidelines to understand their data collection and usage practices in detail. You can access them through the following links:
    *   [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
    *   [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
    *   [Firebase Privacy and Security Guide](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### III. User Feedback

When you contact us through the "Feedback" feature, we receive your email address and the information you provide in the email. This information is used solely to communicate with you, resolve the issues you have raised, and improve our application. We will not use this information for any other purpose, nor will we share it with any third parties.

### IV. Information Storage

All user-generated content (such as notes) in this application is stored on your personal device. We do not store this information on our servers.

### V. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will immediately delete it from our records.

### VI. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
